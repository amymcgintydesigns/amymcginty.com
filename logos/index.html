About me

Building brands people feel long before they see a logo. Fifteen years in, I still love to create. My work goes beyond shaping brand identities and systems into storytelling, capturing the human experience at the intersection of art and technology.

I see the world in color. Letters, numbers, countries and music all arrive for me already tinted, and they always have. Every name has a hue and every year has a mood. It’s how I’ve experienced life for as long as I can remember, and it shapes everything I make.

I’m an art director with ten years as a senior creative at one of the world’s leading technology companies. My work sits where people, design and technology meet. I care about the details most people never notice, because that’s usually where a brand either feels right or doesn’t. I’m serious about craft and I take the work seriously, and I’ve never lost the sense of wonder that got me into this in the first place.

Color isn’t decoration for me, it’s information. It’s how I read a room, build an identity and tell when something is finished. I’m now looking for the next step up, leading bigger ideas and the teams who bring them to life.
